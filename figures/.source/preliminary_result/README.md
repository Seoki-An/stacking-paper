# Outdoor experiment mark masking

Three opaque black vector rectangles cover the visible excavator marks: the boom lettering, main side-panel logo and lettering, and rear brand text. `mark-masks.json` records the rectangles in figure PDF coordinates. These are visual overlays, with the source photo retained for editing.

The masks were added both to slide 2 of the project-level `figures/figures.pptx` and to the original high-resolution figure PDF. `source-masked.pptx` is the editable copy; `before-masks.pdf` is the pre-edit backup and `masked-source.pdf` the masked input to Illustrator.

`../export_adjusted_20261102.jsx` exports EPS. EPS-to-PDF conversion disables image downsampling and supplies the manuscript's `preliminary_result.pdf` and its `-eps-converted-to.pdf` companion. The cropped photograph remains approximately 2239 x 1676 pixels, matching the original crop at native resolution. Final EPS/PDF and the compiled manuscript were visually checked.
