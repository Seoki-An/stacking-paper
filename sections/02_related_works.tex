\section{Related Works} \label{sec:related_works}

\subsection{Robotic Packing and Stone Stacking}
Wang and Hauser~\cite{wang2022binpacking} evaluate pile equilibrium in containers via linear programming, without using equilibrium residuals for pose refinement.

Johns et al.~\cite{johns2020autonomous,johns2023framework} combine masonry rules, candidate contact-force optimization, registration, and simulation for freestanding wall construction, but pose updates do not account for load redistribution across the structure.

Liu et al.~\cite{liu2021drystacking} filter simulation-settled poses geometrically; Liu and Napp~\cite{liu2023stability} additionally evaluate structural strength through shake tests, without searching future placements.
Menezes et al.~\cite{menezes2021rocks} learn placement values reflecting future rewards with fixed object sequences and orientations.
We instead combine whole-structure equilibrium-guided pose refinement with search over object choices and placement poses.

\subsection{Contact-Aware Pose Optimization}
Lee et al.~\cite{lee2025contactfactorgraph} use differentiable contact factors and the envelope theorem for configuration inference after force optimization under a predefined task skeleton.
GraspQP~\cite{zurbrugg2025graspqp} refines grasps through KKT differentiation of a force-closure QP.
BODex~\cite{chen2025bodex} couples bilevel grasp optimization with batched GPU contact-force solves using ReLU-QP.
Its contact refinement uses finite differences or detached contact points, whereas we differentiate DSF contact geometry at regular solutions.

\subsection{Simulation-Based Sequential Manipulation Planning}
PMBS~\cite{huang2022pmbs} combines parallel MCTS with GPU simulation for object retrieval from clutter.
We adopt batched evaluation, adding parallel whole-structure contact-force optimization for pose refinement before simulation at each expansion.
