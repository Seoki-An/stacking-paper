\section{Related Works} \label{sec:related_works}

\subsection{Robotic Packing and Stone Stacking}
Packing and stacking combine geometric criteria with stability assessment.
For container packing, Wang and Hauser~\cite{wang2022binpacking} evaluate pile equilibrium via linear programming.
For freestanding walls, Johns et al.~\cite{johns2020autonomous,johns2023framework} combine masonry rules, candidate contact force optimization, geometric alignment, and simulation.
Liu et al.~\cite{liu2021drystacking} geometrically filter simulation-settled poses, with Liu and Napp~\cite{liu2023stability} adding shake tests for structural strength.
Menezes et al.~\cite{menezes2021rocks} learn placement values reflecting future rewards with fixed object sequences and orientations.

\subsection{Contact-Aware Pose Optimization}
Bilevel manipulation and grasp planners refine configurations using optimized contact forces.
Lee et al.~\cite{lee2025contactfactorgraph} use contact factors and the envelope theorem under a predefined task skeleton.
GraspQP~\cite{zurbrugg2025graspqp} differentiates the Karush--Kuhn--Tucker (KKT) conditions of a force-closure quadratic program.
BODex~\cite{chen2025bodex} batches GPU force solves, using finite differences for coarse contact derivatives and holding link-frame contact coordinates fixed during fine-stage differentiation.
We differentiate DSF contact geometry at regular solutions to account for force and torque equilibrium across the entire structure.

\subsection{Simulation-Based Sequential Manipulation Planning}
PMBS~\cite{huang2022pmbs} batches GPU physics simulations during MCTS expansion and rollout for object retrieval from clutter.
We adopt batched expansion, adding parallel pose refinement with contact force optimization for the entire structure before simulation.
