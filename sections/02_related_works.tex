\section{Related Works} \label{sec:related_works}

\subsection{Automated Stone Stacking}
Prior stone-stacking methods combine pose search with simulation~\cite{furrer2017stacking} or use geometric placement rules for wall construction~\cite{johns2020autonomous,johns2023framework,liu2021drystacking}.
Liu and Napp~\cite{liu2023stability} incorporate structural strength into sequence planning.
Menezes et al.~\cite{menezes2021rocks} learn placement values with a fixed object sequence and fixed orientations, and their elevation-map strategy informs our initial pose sampler.
We combine target-driven pose optimization and structural equilibrium evaluation with explicit lookahead over object choices and placement poses.

\subsection{Contact-Aware Pose Optimization}
BODex~\cite{chen2025bodex} couples lower-level contact-force optimization with upper-level grasp synthesis, while GraspQP~\cite{zurbrugg2025graspqp} defines a differentiable force-closure energy through a quadratic program.
Our optimizer couples candidate placement with structure-wide equilibrium, using bodywise force decomposition for parallel evaluation.

\subsection{Simulation-Based Sequential Manipulation Planning}
Parallel Monte Carlo Tree Search with Batched Simulations (PMBS)~\cite{huang2022pmbs} couples parallel search with GPU rigid-body simulation for object retrieval from clutter.
Our stacking planner adopts batched evaluation, integrating pose refinement and structural simulation at each expansion.
